# Sentence-level sentiment guide
positive: explicit favourable attitude toward the declared target.
negative: explicit unfavourable attitude toward the declared target.
neutral: factual statement without a clear evaluative attitude.
Exclude or adjudicate mixed sentiment ('good teaching, terrible support'), sarcasm, missing context and uncertain target. The exclusion log is useful work.
Examples (illustrative, organiser-authored):
positive: 'Huduma ilikuwa nzuri sana.' / 'The service was excellent.'
negative: 'Nimesikitishwa na huduma hii.' / 'I am disappointed with this service.'
neutral: 'Ofisi inafunguliwa saa mbili asubuhi.' / 'The office opens in the morning.'
Do not label based on positive/negative words alone: negation changes meaning. Do not translate English sentiment labels into incompatible local categories without documenting a clear mapping.
