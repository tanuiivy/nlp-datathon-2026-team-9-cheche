# Completed teaching sentiment guide
Version:example v2.0. Unit:one sentence about a declared service.
positive:clear approval. Example:Huduma ilikuwa nzuri sana.
negative:clear disapproval. Example:Nimesikitishwa na huduma hii.
neutral:factual statement without clear judgement. Example:Ofisi inafunguliwa asubuhi.
Boundary:Huduma haikuwa nzuri expresses negative judgement despite containing nzuri.
Ambiguous/mixed:Walimu ni wazuri lakini huduma ni mbaya. Exclude or adjudicate; do not force a label.
Examples illustrate the rule only; a fluent reviewer must confirm local wording. Sentiment is about attitude;Kuna mvua kesho? is a question,not inherently negative.
Review:another competent person checks the reserved test examples; record disagreements and their resolution.
